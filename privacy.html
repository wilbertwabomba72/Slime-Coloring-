Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Slime Coloring handles information when you use the app.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Slime Coloring is designed as an offline single-player puzzle game. We do not require account registration, and we do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Local Device Storage

The game stores gameplay progress, settings, and related save data locally on your device so you can continue playing later. This local data is not transmitted to us.

3. No Mandatory Internet Connection

Slime Coloring does not require an internet connection to play. Core gameplay, level progress, and settings are available offline.

4. Third-Party Services

At the time of writing, Slime Coloring does not rely on third-party analytics, account systems, or advertising SDKs for core gameplay.

5. Children’s Privacy

Slime Coloring is intended as a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Deletion

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you can remove stored data by deleting the app or clearing the app’s local data where supported by your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes are made, the updated version will be reflected in a revised policy page.

8. Contact

If you have privacy questions about Slime Coloring, please use the support contact information provided on the app’s App Store product page.
